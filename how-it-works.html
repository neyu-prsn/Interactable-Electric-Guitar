A closer look · electromagnetic pickup

Motion becomes signal.

Move the steel string above the pickup. Its motion changes the magnetic flux through the coil; that changing flux induces a small alternating voltage, which can travel through the cable to an amplifier.

01 / THE STRING

Vibrating steel

A pluck makes the ferromagnetic string move across the pickup’s magnetic field. The string itself does not produce the electrical signal.

02 / THE PICKUP

Magnet + coil

A permanent magnet magnetizes the nearby string. A coil of many wire turns surrounds the pickup’s pole pieces. As the string moves, magnetic flux through those turns changes.

Faraday’s law, in plain language: changing magnetic flux through a coil induces voltage in its windings. A stationary string gives no continuing vibration signal; faster motion creates a larger induced voltage in this simplified model.
